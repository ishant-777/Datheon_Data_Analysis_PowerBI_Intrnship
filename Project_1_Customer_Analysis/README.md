Customer Purchase Volume, Monthly Seasonality, Category-wise Revenue and Discount Sensitivity Analysis

Objective
Analyze customer purchasing behavior, seasonal trends, category-wise revenue contribution, and the impact of discounts on sales and profitability.

Tools Used
Python
Pandas
NumPy
Matplotlib
Seaborn

Key Findings
November recorded the highest sales and order volume.
Technology generated the highest category revenue.
High-frequency buyers contributed significantly to total revenue.
Higher discounts increased sales but reduced profitability.
